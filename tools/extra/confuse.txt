# 易混淆字。格式:
#   ## 組名|類型          (類型:拼字相近 / 語意相近)
#   note: 一句辨析重點(比較卡上方的「用法」;答題後的解析也用它)
#   hide: 1             (選填:比較卡上方不顯示 note,因為只是重複下方中文釋義)
#   tip: 記憶口訣        (選填:分號換行顯示)
#   word|詞性|中文       (成員;缺詞性中文時自動從 gsat.json 補)
#   ? 答案|含 ___ 的句子|要排除的選項(可省略,空白分隔)
## empty / vacant / hollow / blank / open|語意相近
note: empty = 裡面沒有東西;vacant = 沒人占用、可使用;hollow = 內部中空;blank = 表面空白;open = 開放、尚可參與。
empty|adj.|空的
vacant|adj.|無人使用的、空缺的
hollow|adj.|中空的
blank|adj.|空白的
open|adj.|開著的、開放的
? empty|The glass is ___, so please fill it with water.|vacant open
? vacant|The hospital has no ___ beds tonight.|empty open
? hollow|The old tree trunk is ___ inside, and a family of owls lives there.|empty vacant
? blank|Please leave this page ___ so I can draw on it.|empty open
? open|Applications are still ___, so you can apply until Friday.|vacant empty
## relatable / relative|拼字相近

relatable|adj.|令人產生共鳴的

? relatable|That video about being tired all the time was so ___.
? relative|We visited our ___ in Taichung last weekend.

? relatable|Many students find the main character very ___ because they feel the same pressure.

## suspend / cease / stop / hold / abort|語意相近
note: suspend = 暫停(可能恢復);cease = 正式的停止;stop = 一般的停止;hold = 暫緩;abort = 在完成前中止。

? suspend|The ferry service was ___ed because of the typhoon, and will start again tomorrow.|cease stop hold abort

## affect / effect|拼字相近
note: affect 是動詞(影響);effect 是名詞(效果、影響),have an effect on = affect。
tip: A = Action(動作 → 動詞);E = End result(結果 → 名詞)
affect|v.|影響
effect|n.|效果、影響
? affect|The loud music did not ___ her concentration.
? effect|The new medicine had a quick ___ on his cough.
? effect|Pollution has a bad ___ on our health.
? affect|Lack of sleep can ___ your grades.
